# Ethics and safety checklist

- [ ] Institutional ethics approval is in place before participant data collection.
- [ ] Participants receive an understandable information sheet and provide informed consent.
- [ ] Participant codes are used; no names or direct identifiers are stored with research data.
- [ ] No face video is retained; landmark files contain only the hand coordinates needed.
- [ ] The EMG setup follows the manufacturer's instructions and is battery powered while connected to a participant.
- [ ] Participants can pause or stop; stop for discomfort or skin irritation.
- [ ] Data access, storage, retention, and deletion follow institutional policy.
- [ ] Reports state that this is a research prototype, not a certified medical device or treatment recommendation.
